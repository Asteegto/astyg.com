Lifts

Planning a building with three or more floors? Plan the elevator early.

Elevators affect your building design, budget and permit. Get it wrong and you're stuck with long waits, a shaft that doesn't fit, or a lift that can't carry a stretcher. We help you plan it before construction starts.

Does this sound familiar?

If any of these is you, you're in the right place.

You're designing a 3-storey or taller buildingand don't know how many lifts or what size you need.
You run a clinic or hospitaland need a lift that fits a stretcher or wheelchair.
You're comparing elevator quotationsand every supplier says something different.
Your existing lift is old or keeps breakingand you want an honest assessment.

What we handle for you

  • How many lifts, what size and what speed
  • Shaft and machine room requirements for your architect
  • A specification so suppliers quote the same thing
  • Inspection and evaluation of existing lifts

What happens if it's done wrong

  • A shaft built too small for the lift you need
  • Long waiting times every day
  • Paying for a lift that's bigger and faster than necessary

Common questions

The elevator company will design it for free. Why pay you?

They'll design around their own product. We make sure what you buy fits your building and needs, and help you compare quotes fairly.

When should I involve you?

Before the architect finalizes the plans. Moving a shaft later is expensive.

Not sure if this is the service I need?

Just tell us your problem in your own words. If it's not lifts, we'll point you to the right service, or the right person.
